GIGA CAT Gold Fullscreen Patch v5

What it changes:
- Replaces the purple card fill with a warm gold gradient overlay
- Stretches the visible GIGA CAT card to full screen height
- Makes the background image fill the card vertically

What it does NOT change:
- No content deletion
- No automatic restart

Install:
unzip -o Giga-Cat-Gold-Fullscreen-Patch-v5.zip && bash Giga-Cat-Gold-Fullscreen-Patch-v5/install.sh

Restore:
bash Giga-Cat-Gold-Fullscreen-Patch-v5/restore-latest.sh
